# ApexPathAI Community Guidelines

## Our Commitment
ApexPathAI is dedicated to providing an open, inclusive, and professional environment for researchers, software engineers, roboticists, and students contributing to autonomous vehicle technology.

## Expected Behavior
- Engage with fellow contributors respectfully, constructively, and professionally.
- Provide objective, technically reasoned feedback on algorithms, code, and documentation.
- Welcome contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds.
- Focus on collective technical excellence, mathematical rigor, and safety integrity.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Demeaning, harassing, or discriminatory speech and behavior.
- Personal attacks or trolling during code reviews or community discussions.
- Publishing private identifying information without consent.

## Enforcement & Reporting
Community participants who observe behavior violating these standards may report incidents to the project maintainers via email at `conduct@apexpath.ai`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
