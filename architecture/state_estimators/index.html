    State Estimation Mathematical Formulations

    ApexPathAI incorporates two complementary state estimators: 1. Planar 5-State CTRV EKF (LocalizationEKF) 2. 3D 15-DOF Error-State Kalman Filter on \(\text{SE}(3) \times \mathbb{R}^6\) (ErrorStateKalmanFilter3D)

    1. Planar CTRV Extended Kalman Filter

    State Vector

    The state vector is defined in the vehicle navigation plane: $\(x = \begin{bmatrix} p_x & p_y & \theta & v & \omega \end{bmatrix}^T \in \mathbb{R}^2 \times S^1 \times \mathbb{R}^2\)$

    Where: - \(p_x, p_y\): Global Cartesian position (meters) - \(\theta\): Heading / yaw angle normalized to \([-\pi, \pi)\) (radians) - \(v\): Longitudinal linear velocity (m/s) - \(\omega\): Yaw rate (rad/s)

    CTRV Motion Model Integration

    For non-zero yaw rate (\(|\omega| > 10^{-5}\,\text{rad/s}\)), circular-arc integration is used:

    \[\begin{aligned} p_x(t + \Delta t) &= p_x(t) + \frac{v}{\omega} \Big(\sin(\theta + \omega \Delta t) - \sin(\theta)\Big) \\ p_y(t + \Delta t) &= p_y(t) + \frac{v}{\omega} \Big(-\cos(\theta + \omega \Delta t) + \cos(\theta)\Big) \\ \theta(t + \Delta t) &= \text{wrap}\Big(\theta(t) + \omega \Delta t\Big) \\ v(t + \Delta t) &= v(t) \\ \omega(t + \Delta t) &= \omega(t) \end{aligned}\]

    For \(|\omega| \le 10^{-5}\,\text{rad/s}\), a second-order midpoint Taylor expansion prevents division by zero: $\(\begin{aligned} \theta_{\text{mid}} &= \theta + \frac{1}{2} \omega \Delta t \\ p_x(t + \Delta t) &= p_x(t) + v \cos(\theta_{\text{mid}}) \Delta t \\ p_y(t + \Delta t) &= p_y(t) + v \sin(\theta_{\text{mid}}) \Delta t \end{aligned}\)$

    Continuous-to-Discrete Process Noise Projection

    Process noise accounts for longitudinal acceleration noise \(\sigma_a^2\) and angular acceleration noise \(\sigma_\alpha^2\): $\(Q(\theta, \omega, \Delta t) = \Gamma \, Q_u \, \Gamma^T + Q_{\text{base}} \frac{\Delta t}{\Delta t_{\text{ref}}}\)$

    \[\Gamma = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{2} \Delta t^2 \cos(\theta_{\text{mid}}) & 0 \\ \frac{1}{2} \Delta t^2 \sin(\theta_{\text{mid}}) & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{2} \Delta t^2 \\ \Delta t & 0 \\ 0 & \Delta t \end{bmatrix}, \quad Q_u = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma_a^2 & 0 \\ 0 & \sigma_\alpha^2 \end{bmatrix}\]

    Joseph Stabilized Measurement Update

    To guarantee that the updated error covariance remains symmetric positive semi-definite (\(P = P^T \ge 0\)): $\(P_{k|k} = (I - K_k H_k) P_{k|k-1} (I - K_k H_k)^T + K_k R_k K_k^T\)$

    2. 15-DOF Error-State Kalman Filter on \(\text{SE}(3) \times \mathbb{R}^6\)

    Nominal and Error States

    • Nominal State (16-D): $\(x_{\text{nom}} = \begin{bmatrix} p_{3\times 1} & v_{3\times 1} & q_{4\times 1} & b_{a, 3\times 1} & b_{\omega, 3\times 1} \end{bmatrix}^T\)$ Where \(q\) is a unit quaternion representing attitude on \(\text{SO}(3)\).
    • Error State (15-DOF): $\(\delta x = \begin{bmatrix} \delta p & \delta v & \delta \theta & \delta b_a & \delta b_\omega \end{bmatrix}^T \in \mathbb{R}^{15}\)$ Where \(\delta \theta \in \mathfrak{so}(3)\) is the rotation vector in the Lie algebra tangent space.

    Strapdown Inertial Mechanization

    Given accelerometer measurement \(a_m\) and gyroscope measurement \(\omega_m\): $\(a_b = a_m - b_a, \quad \omega_b = \omega_m - b_\omega\)$ $\(a_g = R(q) a_b + g_{\text{ENU}}\)$ $\(\dot{p} = v, \quad \dot{v} = a_g, \quad \dot{q} = \frac{1}{2} q \otimes \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \omega_b \end{bmatrix}\)$

    Manifold State Injection & Error Reset

    When a measurement update produces error state \(\delta x\): $\(\begin{aligned} p &\leftarrow p + \delta p \\ v &\leftarrow v + \delta v \\ q &\leftarrow q \otimes \text{Exp}(\delta \theta) \\ b_a &\leftarrow b_a + \delta b_a \\ b_\omega &\leftarrow b_\omega + \delta b_\omega \end{aligned}\)$ $\(\delta x \leftarrow 0\)$
